# Changelog

## 0.1.0 - 2026-11-24

First public release.

- Live terminal panel with the signal margin, a countdown to the drop and a chart for every Mac and iPad that Universal Control can see.
- Detail view, line-by-line output (`--plain`) and replay of a recorded log (`--replay`).
- Thresholds checked against the `nearbyd` decisions in the local log.
- Interface in 15 languages.
